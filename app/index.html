Liberty Cut

A cinematic studio built entirely on open weights — LTX video, FLUX stills, PuLID identity. No partner APIs.

Checking ComfyUI…

1 · Cast

Create a cast card for each original, fictional character. The card becomes the identity reference for every shot — the same face, locked across the whole scene. Generated imagery only; never a real person's likeness.

2 · Ensemble Continuity

Write a shot list (three or more shots). Each shot is keyframed with its character's identity locked, animated by LTX with the camera move you choose, then assembled into one scene.


    

  


  
  

    
3 · Relight & Hour

    
Take any keyframe from a rendered shot and re-light it at a different hour —
       identity re-locked, composition kept — then animate the new hour.
